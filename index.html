About the Conference

Where Innovation Meets Clinical Excellence

REMINISCENCE 3.0 brings together the brightest minds in Interventional Pulmonology for a transformative day of learning, innovation, and collaboration — hosted at the prestigious Narayana Health City, Bengaluru.

Our Vision

To foster a culture of excellence in Interventional Pulmonology by bridging the gap between cutting-edge research and everyday clinical practice — across India and beyond.

Objectives

  • Advance knowledge in bronchoscopic techniques
  • Share emerging evidence based case experiences
  • Develop skills through interactive sessions
  • Foster inter-institutional collaboration
  • Inspire the next generation of IP practitioners

Why Interventional Pulmonology?

Interventional Pulmonology is one of the fastest-growing subspecialties in medicine. From EBUS-TBNA to robotic bronchoscopy, This is to transform how we diagnose and treat respiratory disease with minimally invasive procedure with greater precision than ever before.
